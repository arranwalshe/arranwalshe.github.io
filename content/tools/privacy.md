  <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>

  <p>This policy covers the personal automation tools described on the <a style="color: white;" href="/tools/">tools page</a>. They are used only by Arran Walshe, on his own Google account.</p>

  <h2>What data is accessed</h2>
  <p>The tools access only the account owner's own Google data, such as Google Docs, Drive and Sheets, through the scopes each Google Cloud project is granted. They do not access anyone else's account.</p>

  <h2>How data is used</h2>
  <p>The data is used only to carry out tasks the owner asks for, such as reading, editing, formatting or exporting his own documents. It is not used for advertising or profiling.</p>

  <h2>How data is stored and shared</h2>
  <p>OAuth tokens stay on the owner's own computers. At the owner's direction, document content may be processed by AI tools he uses, including Anthropic's Claude, to carry out those tasks. Content processed this way is not used to train AI models. Otherwise it is never shared, sold or sent to third parties.</p>

  <h2>Google API Services User Data Policy</h2>
  <p>Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the <a style="color: white;" href="https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy">Google API Services User Data Policy</a>, including the Limited Use requirements.</p>

  <h2>Revoking access</h2>
  <p>Access can be revoked at any time at <a style="color: white;" href="https://myaccount.google.com/permissions">myaccount.google.com/permissions</a>.</p>
